MATERIAL MODELLING / PACKAGING

Material Characterisation & Nonlinear Simulation

Paper testing, LS-DYNA modelling and LS-OPT calibration for packaging.

Project overview

My master’s thesis at BSH investigated honeycomb paperboard for dishwasher packaging through paper characterisation and board compression testing.

  • LS-DYNA
  • MAT 58 / MAT 274
  • LS-OPT
  • Surrogate modelling

Approach

  • Characterise liner and core paper in tension and compression.
  • Compare MAT 58 and MAT 274 in LS-DYNA; calibrate MAT 58 parameters using LS-OPT.

Key findings

  • MAT 274 broadly reproduced the board’s crushing plateau.
  • Differences remained during early crushing and densification.

The LS-OPT calibration method is documented; its final improvement is not quantified.

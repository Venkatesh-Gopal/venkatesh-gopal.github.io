BONDED STRUCTURES / MECHANICAL AND THERMAL ANALYSIS

Structural & Thermomechanical Analysis

Investigating load transfer and deformation in bonded structures under mechanical and thermal loading, with steel reinforcement as the application.

My contribution

My documented scope covered mechanical and thermal–mechanical FEM. The study concerns load transfer and deformation in bonded structures; published strain measurements provide the experimental research context.

  • FEM
  • Bonded structures
  • Thermal loading
  • Structural mechanics

Project at a glance

Item Scope
Application Bonded steel-patch reinforcement of steel structures.
Analysis scope Mechanical and thermal–mechanical finite element modelling.
Research context Distributed fibre optic sensing of strain in a repaired structure.
Engineering question How do loading, load transfer and local damage affect the repair’s response?

Findings from the experimental context

Observation Engineering relevance
Load redistribution Published strain measurements illustrate how the response varies along the bonded repair.
Progressive damage Published observations show damage development that must be considered when interpreting local strain changes.

Engineering focus

Connect structural response with the mechanics of the bonded repair. Distinguish changes caused by loading from those associated with damage when interpreting the measurements.

The observations above are published experimental context. The supplied project material contains no numerical FEM result plots or quantified model-validation outcome.

COMPUTATIONAL FRACTURE

Fracture Modelling & Failure Assessment

Abaqus XFEM study of crack growth in fibre-reinforced adhesives.

Project overview

Compared experimental and simulated stress–strain responses and crack paths at four fibre orientations: 0°, 30°, 60° and 90°.

  • Abaqus
  • XFEM
  • Fracture mechanics
  • Material behaviour

Approach

  • Analyse a notched adhesive specimen with 3D Abaqus XFEM.
  • Compare tensile response and crack direction across the four orientations.

Key findings

  • Simulated failure strains were lower than the experimental values.
  • Angled crack paths at 30° and 60° were not reproduced.

Published specimen study; the source credits J. Philipp with the XFEM simulation.

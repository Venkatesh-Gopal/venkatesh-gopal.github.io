COMPUTATIONAL FRACTURE

Fracture in fibre-reinforced adhesives

XFEM analysis of fracture behaviour in fibre-reinforced adhesives.

Project overview

This project explores fibre-reinforced adhesives through extended finite element analysis (XFEM). The focus is on fracture behaviour and the use of numerical modelling to investigate the mechanical response of adhesive materials.

  • XFEM
  • Adhesives
  • Fracture mechanics

Research focus

  • Fibre-reinforced adhesives as the material system.
  • Extended finite element analysis (XFEM).
  • Investigation of fracture and mechanical behaviour.

COMPUTATIONAL FRACTURE

Understanding fracture in reinforced adhesives

Exploring crack initiation and propagation through extended finite element modelling.

The engineering question

How can numerical modelling help explain where cracking begins and how it develops in a reinforced adhesive material?

Project overview

This project investigates the fracture behaviour of fibre-reinforced adhesives using the extended finite element method, or XFEM. The focus is on examining the mechanical response of adhesive materials and the development of cracks.

  • XFEM
  • Abaqus
  • Adhesives
  • Fracture mechanics

Technical focus

  • Examine the mechanical response of the adhesive system under loading.
  • Use XFEM to investigate crack initiation and propagation.
  • Interpret the predicted response alongside the assumptions behind the analysis.

Why it matters

Understanding fracture helps engineers evaluate how adhesive systems carry load and where failure may develop. The model becomes useful when its predictions are connected with the material, loading conditions, and evidence available.

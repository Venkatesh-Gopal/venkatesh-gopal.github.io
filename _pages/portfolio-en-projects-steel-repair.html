STRUCTURAL AND THERMAL ANALYSIS

Structural & Thermomechanical Analysis

Load transfer, defect sensing and thermal response of bonded steel reinforcement.

Project overview

Mechanical and thermal–mechanical FEM of bonded steel reinforcement, interpreted alongside distributed fibre optic strain measurements.

  • FEM
  • Bonded structures
  • Thermal loading
  • Structural mechanics

Approach

  • Investigate load transfer and deformation in the bonded repair.
  • Compare temperature histories at T1–T4 under thermal loading.

Key findings

  • Strain profiles show load redistribution and local changes associated with bond defects.
  • Temperature histories compare experimental and simulated thermal responses.

The published strain measurements provide the experimental research context.

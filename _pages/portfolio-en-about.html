ABOUT / VENKATESH GOPAL

From machine design
to computational engineering.

My engineering career began with designing load-bearing machine structures. It developed through industrial simulation at BSH and applied research at TU Braunschweig, where I investigated structural behaviour, materials, and failure.

I hold an M.Sc. in Computational Sciences in Engineering from TU Braunschweig, Germany. That background connects practical mechanical engineering with numerical methods, experimental investigation, and programming.

I bring these perspectives together when approaching a problem: how the component is designed, how the material behaves, how the model represents reality, and what the evidence supports.

My contribution also extends to the engineering process itself. I develop Python tools for CAE workflows and apply surrogate-based optimisation to material calibration.

I value technical ownership: asking the right questions, making assumptions clear, and explaining what the findings mean for the next design decision.

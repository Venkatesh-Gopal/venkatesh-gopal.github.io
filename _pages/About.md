I am Venkatesh Gopal, a mechanical engineer working in finite element analysis and computational mechanics. I hold an MSc in Computational Sciences in Engineering from TU Braunschweig and have worked there as a Research Associate.

My projects combine numerical modelling with experimental comparison. They include monitoring steel patch repairs with distributed fibre optic sensing, XFEM analysis of fibre-reinforced adhesives, and mechanical analysis of honeycomb paperboard.

I am interested in FEM and structural analysis opportunities in the Netherlands and Germany. I speak fluent English and German at B2 level, and I am learning Dutch.

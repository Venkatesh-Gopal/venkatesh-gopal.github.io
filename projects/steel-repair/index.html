Monitoring steel patch repairs

Distributed fibre optic sensing applied to steel patch repairs.

Project overview

This project concerns monitoring steel patch repairs using distributed fibre optic sensing. It brings together structural mechanics and experimental observations to investigate the behaviour of repaired steel structures.

  • Steel repairs
  • Fibre optic sensing
  • Experimental comparison

Research focus

  • Steel patch repairs as a structural engineering application.
  • Distributed fibre optic sensing for monitoring.
  • Interpretation of experimental observations in a structural mechanics context.

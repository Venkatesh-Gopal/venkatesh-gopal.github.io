LIGHTWEIGHT STRUCTURES

Mechanics of honeycomb paperboard

Mechanical analysis of a lightweight cellular material.

Project overview

This project investigates the mechanical response of honeycomb paperboard. It connects the behaviour of a cellular structure with questions in structural mechanics and numerical modelling.

  • Cellular structures
  • Paperboard
  • Mechanical response

Research focus

  • Honeycomb paperboard as a lightweight structural material.
  • Mechanical response under loading.
  • The relationship between cellular geometry and structural behaviour.
